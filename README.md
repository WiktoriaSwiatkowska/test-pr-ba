## INTRODUCTION
Krakow is one of the biggest and most visited cities in Poland. Every day, thousands of people use public transport to travel between different parts of the city. The system includes both trams and buses, which are known for being frequent and affordable.

Public transport in Krakow is managed by MPK Krakow. It provides many lines that connect important areas such as the Main Square, Kazimierz, Nowa Huta and Bronowice. The trams and buses usually run from early morning until late night, and there are also several night lines for people working or going out late.

The city keeps improving its transport network by buying modern trams and eco-friendly buses. New routes and ticket machines are being added every year. Thanks to this, travelling around Krakow has become faster and more comfortable for both locals and tourists.
